Welcome to The Zavila Galaxy Official Wiki

A green and purple archive for the characters, stories, and events of The Zavila Galaxy— an expanded universe born from the Rise of Super mythos and pushed into full galactic scale.

What Is The Zavila Galaxy?

The Zavila Galaxy is the primary setting for a Roblox RP universe that extends the original Rise of Super storyline into a vast cosmic frontier. Old faces return, new factions rise, and entire star systems become battlegrounds for power, legacy, and survival.

This page acts as a compact hub: quick character references, key stories, and a rough timeline of major events so far.
